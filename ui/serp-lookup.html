SERP Look Up

Paste or upload a CSV of people — columns First Name, Last Name, Employer, Website, Title (header row optional). For each person we run one Google search (serper.dev) and return their LinkedIn URL, a Bio URL (on the employer's site, else a matching bio/contact page), and the result snippet. Then optionally push the bio URLs through extraction to get full contact records.
